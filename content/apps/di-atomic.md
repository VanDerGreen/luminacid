# 02 // THE PROTOCOL

&nbsp;

### ◈ CHAIN REACTION ENGINE
Connect 3 or more atoms of the same color. Once they fuse, they vanish, triggering massive gravity-defying combos. Set up your grid to witness the domino effect of pure physics.

&nbsp;

### ◈ TACTICAL ROTATION
You aren't just dropping pieces; you’re maneuvering them. Swap between your current and next piece or rotate your atoms mid-air to find the perfect fit. Strategy is your only shield.

&nbsp;

### ◈ HAPTIC SENSORS
Every match is felt. Fully integrated haptics and industrial sound effects make every move feel tactile and intentional. You are operating a machine.
