# 01 // THE ARCHITECT'S VISION

&nbsp;

### BEYOND BIOLOGY: THE DIGITAL GARDEN
**DotLife** is not a game; it is a petri dish for the 21st century. It simulates emergent AI-driven life within a volatile, dynamic environment. Here, you aren't just a spectator—you are the Architect of an ecosystem where every pixel has a pulse and every entity learns to survive.

# 02 // CORE PROTOCOLS

&nbsp;

### ◈ NEURAL ADAPTATION
Watch as entities develop unique behavioral traits. Through real-time decision-making trees, these digital organisms learn from their environment, finding food, avoiding predators, and forming complex social structures.

&nbsp;

### ◈ EVOLUTIONARY ACCELERATION
Witness centuries of biological progress condensed into seconds. Experiment with mutation rates and environmental pressures to see which lineages dominate and which vanish into the source code.

&nbsp;

### ◈ ENVIRONMENTAL REACTIVITY
The ecosystem is alive. Change the climate, deplete resources, or introduce new predators. The simulation reacts instantly, forcing your "Dots" to evolve or evaporate.
